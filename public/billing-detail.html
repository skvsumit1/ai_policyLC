Invoice Lifecycle
1. Invoice Generated
System creates the invoice at the billing cycle anchor date, recording the period, premium amount, and due date. A creation timestamp is logged for audit.
2. Invoice Exported & Delivered
Packaged as a PDF and/or EDI 810 transaction and sent via member portal, email, or mail. Delivery is timestamped to start the payment window clock.
3. Payment Window Open
Policyholder submits remittance on or before the due date. Premium must be received to keep coverage active through the billing period.
4. Payment Posted
Received payment is matched to this invoice and posted to the billing ledger. Status changes to Paid and a receipt timestamp is stamped on the record.
5. Reconciled
Finance runs daily/monthly reconciliation to match all received payments against open invoices in the billing system. Discrepancies are flagged before the books are closed.
Overdue Path Active
The dunning process has begun — escalating reminders are sent at Day 1, Day 15, and Day 30 past due. Continued non-payment ages the invoice and may trigger a policy lapse . Persistent balances are written off and may be referred to collections.

EDI Transactions: Large group invoices are transmitted as EDI 810 transactions through a clearinghouse . Payments return as EDI 835 / ERA , which auto-post in provider billing systems.

Premium Calculation: Your monthly premium is actuarially priced at policy issuance and does not change mid-term unless you update coverage. Factors include age band, plan tier, and geographic rating area.

Aging & Write-Offs: Unpaid invoices are tracked in an aging report (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days). After 90+ days, balances may be written off and the account referred to a collections agency.
